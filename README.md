A neon salvage run in the shadow of a dead planet. Pilot a recovery drone through a collapsing debris ring, chain glowing scrap for score, and spend pulse energy to clear a path. Reach 1,500 contract credits before the storm strips your hull.

## How to play

- **WASD / arrow keys:** thrust in eight directions
- **Mouse:** aim the drone and pulse reticle
- **Click / Space:** fire a pulse that breaks nearby debris (35 energy; energy recharges)
- **P / Esc:** pause or resume
- **M:** mute or unmute synthesized sound
- **Enter / click:** launch or restart

Collect cyan scrap, rare gold caches, and temporary SHIELD, MAGNET, or OVERDRIVE systems. Consecutive salvage increases the score multiplier. Three hull impacts end the contract.

Run locally with `node serve.ts` and open http://127.0.0.1:8080. The game is static and loads Three.js from its local vendor file.
